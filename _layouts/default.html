My research lies at the intersection of law, finance, and trust, with a focus on how legal structures and enforcement mechanisms shape pricing, risk, and behavior in debt markets. I specialize in the empirical analysis of complex, unstructured public data—including bond covenants, trust arrangements, litigation records, and regulatory disclosures—to uncover under-appreciated sources of market friction, asymmetry, and institutional risk. My work aims to inform both academic theory and policy by revealing how legal opacity, strategic behavior, and enforcement dynamics influence the design, valuation, and resilience of financial instruments.
